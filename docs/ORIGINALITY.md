# Originality rules — Metal Swan 3

**Metal Swan 3** is an *original* game. It borrows only the **genre mechanics** of 1990s arcade
run-and-gun shooters (which are not protected), never the protected **expression** of any
existing game. Every agent must follow these rules for names, art, text, sound and level design.

## Allowed (genre mechanics and conventions)

- Side-scrolling run-and-gun play, one-hit deaths, lives and continues, score.
- Weapon crates with letter icons and limited ammo; grenades; automatic melee at close range.
- Tied-up captives that the player frees and who hand over items.
- Vehicles the player can enter, with their own weapons and armour; ejecting.
- Branching routes, checkpoints, timers, camera locks for arena fights, multi-part bosses.
- 2-player co-op, comedic tone, cartoon (bloodless) deaths, a military setting. (One dated
  exception to "bloodless": the first-person mode, see "Exception: the first-person mode" below.)
- Generic weapon types and names: pistol, machine gun, rocket launcher, shotgun, flamethrower,
  laser, homing missiles, grenades, knife.
- Generic phrases such as "MISSION 1", "START", "MISSION COMPLETE", "GAME OVER", "CONTINUE?".

## Not allowed (protected expression)

Do not use, imitate or closely paraphrase any of the following from the *Metal Slug* series
(SNK / Nazca) or from any other franchise:

- **Names:** Metal Slug, SNK, Nazca, Marco, Tarma, Eri, Fio, Peregrine Falcons, Sparrows,
  Morden, Rebel Army, Regular Army, Ptolemaic Army, Mars People, Rootmars, Allen O'Neil,
  Hyakutaro, Rumi Aikawa, Huge Hermit, Sol Dae Rokker, Jupiter King, Monoeye, Hi-Do, Big Shiee,
  Tani Oh, Iron Nokana, Aeshi Nero, Girida-O, Di-Cokka, Bradley, Sarubia, SV-001, Slug Flyer,
  Slug Mariner, Elephant Slug, Camel Slug, Ostrich Slug, Donkey Slug, Drill Slug, Slug Copter,
  Astro Slug, LV Armor, or any "<animal> Slug" vehicle naming.
- **Character designs:** no look-alikes of the series' heroes, villains or enemy soldiers (for
  example: no bearded captives in underwear, no green-fatigue soldier with the series' insignia,
  no tentacled alien with a ray gun, no round tank with a top vulcan and the series' silhouette).
  Use the project's own character, faction and vehicle designs from `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`.
- **Signature gags and transformations:** no zombie / mummy / fat transformations and no
  copies of specific set pieces, boss designs or level layouts.
- **Text and voice:** no copied announcer lines, captions, story text or dialogue. Write new lines.
- **Music and sound:** compose new melodies, harmonies and rhythms. Never transcribe or
  "arrange" an existing tune from any game, film or song. No sampled or recorded audio (one
  exception, below: ElevenLabs audio for the start screens, mission `ams` and the first-person
  mode).
- **Art:** never trace, copy, or reproduce sprites, logos, fonts or tiles from any game. All art
  is generated by this project's own code from its own palette.

## Exception: mission `ams` (Amsterdam)

By the user's explicit decision (2026-10-12), mission `ams` uses real-world names: the city of
Amsterdam and its landmarks (Rijksmuseum, Museumplein, Stopera, Amstel, Blauwbrug), the friet
shop Fabel Friet (shown in a friendly light), food-delivery riders in the style of Uber Eats, and
a robot caricature of the city's mayor, Femke Halsema (ROBO-HALSEMA). Rules for this exception:

- All art is drawn by this project's code. Never trace a logo or a photo; shop lettering is our
  own pixel lettering.
- The mayor is a ROBOT caricature with a recognisable likeness (user decision 2026-10-13): her
  big wavy dark-brown hair, her face (strong arched brows, brown eyes, red lips, a confident
  closed smile), a round gold earring, a white bouclé jacket over a red blouse and the silver
  chain of office. The likeness is hand-built from shapes and the palette by our code, studied
  from a reference photo but never traced, sampled or pixel-copied from it. She stays a machine
  (steel neck, joints, hands, a wig over a steel skull); no insults, no human harm.
- The rules above about other games (names, designs, music, text) still apply in full. The
  music for `ams` is original and never quotes an existing song.
- The self-check question 2 below does not apply to these listed real names.
- Weapon announcer lines (user decision 2026-10-14): the pickup voice lines are the plain weapon
  names, and the user chose `HEAVY MACHINE GUN!` (machine gun) and `ENEMY CHASER!` (homing
  missiles), two generic weapon names that the reference series also uses (`MORE <NAME>!` for the
  same weapon again). Only these two short names are excepted; weapon designs, sounds, art and
  all other text stay our own.
- The mayor's voice (2026-10-14): her lines may be spoken by hand-placed clips in
  public/audio/mayor/ (bosses/ams/voice.ts; one file per line, placeholders until the final
  recordings exist).
  Those recordings must be original or made with the speaker's consent: never a cloned,
  sample-trained or imitated voice of the real person. The ElevenLabs tools generate nothing for
  her.

## Exception: generated audio for the start screens and mission `ams`

By the user's decision (2026-10-14), the start screens (boot, title, menus, character select,
briefing) and mission `ams` use audio generated with ElevenLabs (sound effects, music and
dialogue), shipped as MP3 files under `public/audio/`. Rules for this exception:

- The synth recipes and songs stay in the code as the fallback for every id; the other missions
  stay synth-only.
- The first-person mode (2026-10-14) may use the same pipeline (sample group `mode:fps`), with the
  same rules and its own exception below. Its takes are generated only after the user's OK on the
  credit estimate.
- Prompts describe sounds, styles and moods in our own words. Music prompts never name an
  existing song, artist, game or composer to imitate; every cue must be original and must not
  quote an existing melody.
- Voices are designed from text descriptions (ElevenLabs Voice Design). Never clone, name or
  imitate a real person's voice.
- ROBO-HALSEMA never gets a voice: her lines stay text-only bubbles (the code passes
  `voice: false` for them).
- Dialogue text is our own (the game's lines). The generation spec lives in `tools/audio/spec.ts`.
- **The user's own music (2026-10-16).** Four tracks supplied by the user replace the generated
  takes of `title` (title screen and mode select), `select` (REPORT FOR DUTY), `ams` and `ams-boss`
  (`public/audio/user/music/`). The user supplied them for this game and is responsible for their
  rights; we only trimmed them and set their loudness.

## Exception: the squad of friends (2026-10-16)

By the user's explicit decision (2026-10-16, `docs/design/squad.md`), the five playable heroes are
five real friends of the user: VINCENT, THOM, DIRK, BOB and PATRICK. Rules for this exception:

- Only their real first names are used (no surnames). Their bios on the select screen are the
  user's own words, shown as the user wrote them; no insult goes beyond those bios.
- Their faces are kind caricatures drawn by this project's code from shapes and the palette. They
  are studied from the user's reference photos, but never traced, sampled or pixel-copied. The
  photos never enter the repository.
- No ethnic or religious stereotype appears in any art, text or sound.
- The name callouts use a voice designed from a text description (a gruff drill sergeant). No
  voice imitates the friends' real voices, unless a friend records it with consent.
- A bio may mention a real public figure only as the user wrote it; the game never names or
  draws that person.
- The self-check question 2 below does not apply to these five first names.
- The select screen REPORT FOR DUTY follows the genre convention of late-1990s arcade "soldier
  select" screens (a riveted frame, a panel per soldier behind a shutter, grey portraits until
  highlighted, a small sprite preview after the pick, hazard stripes, nameplates, P1 / P2 lamps,
  a countdown). The structure is a convention; the art, the lettering, the squad emblem (a
  stencilled swan head in a chevron with a small 3), the frame and every text are our own. Never
  use the reference game's title text, logo, lettering, frame art, portraits or its wait / start
  lines ("PLEASE WAIT", "SOLDIER SELECT", "MISSION START" and the like).
- The military menu texts (taglines, `SELECT OPERATION`, `RECRUIT` / `VETERAN` / `COMMANDO`,
  `HALL OF FAME`, `LOADING AMMUNITION`) are our own; `tests/game/character-select.test.ts` checks
  them against the banned names above.

## Exception: the first-person mode (2026-10-14)

By the user's decision (2026-10-14), the game turns into a first-person raycaster shooter after
the ams boss (spec: `docs/design/fps.md`). It follows the genre founded by **Wolfenstein 3D** (id
Software, 1992). This is the **only** place where that name may be written: never in the game,
the code, ids, comments, commit messages, prompts or other docs (in-game the mode is
`MODE_TITLE` = "OPERATION OSTIA", the user's name of 2026-10-16). What the user decided:

- **Genre mechanics are allowed:** first-person movement on a tile grid, sliding doors, gold and
  silver keys, push-wall secrets, treasure, health and ammo pickups, a shared ammo pool, knife /
  pistol / machine gun / chaingun, enemies that wake by sight and by gunfire, an elevator exit, a
  kill / secret / treasure tally with a par time, lives and extends. Numbers may follow the genre
  (they are mechanics), but every map, picture, sound and line is ours.
- **The boss name and its two-phase concept are a user-decided exception, not a genre
  convention.** The name "MechaHitler" and the concept "a mech suit, then on foot with two
  chainguns, the wreck stays" follow the reference game's own final boss, by the user's decision
  of 2026-10-14. Only the name and the phase concept are excepted. The look, the lines, the voice
  and the arena stay ours (HP and timings are mechanics and may follow the genre, as above); do not
  stretch this exception to anything else.
- **Historical Nazi symbols** are allowed in this mode only, on banners and flags only (the scope
  the user approved: "swastika banners and flags"; not on uniforms, not on the boss). The enemies
  are the villains; the symbols are never shown in a positive light.
- **Red blood** is allowed in this mode only (`docs/ART_GUIDE.md` section 10). The rest of the
  game stays bloodless.
- **MechaHitler** is a caricature of a historical figure with an original design (a walking
  speaker's podium with brass loudspeaker horns, spec §7.1). His voice is **designed** (ElevenLabs
  Voice Design from a text description of a whiny cartoon tyrant): never a clone, never a sample
  of the real person, never an imitation of real recordings or speeches. His lines are our own
  comic lines, never quotes of the real person.
- No Holocaust imagery, no camps, no ovens, no real victims, no real quotes or slogans.
- **The suit's face and Dutch lines (user decision 2026-10-16, spec §0 item 10, §7.1, §8.7).**
  The phase-1 suit wears pixel art the user supplied ("Mecha Hofmans"), imported once from
  `art-src/user/` and mapped to the palette by `tools/art/import-face.ts`. This is the only raster
  art in the game; the user is responsible for the rights and for the consent of anyone the art
  shows. The suit's three Dutch lines are the user's own text, spoken by a **designed** voice
  (`MS3 Mecha Suit`: a description of a stern Dutch teacher-type baritone, then a robot filter),
  never a clone or an imitation of a real person's voice. The name `MECHAHITLER` and phase 2 stay
  unchanged.

Still banned — do not copy, trace or closely paraphrase anything from Wolfenstein 3D, its
episodes and sequels:

- **Names:** the game's name and its studio's names in the game; B.J. Blazkowicz, Castle
  Wolfenstein, Hans Grosse, Gretel Grosse, Dr. Schabbs, Otto Giftmacher, General Fettgesicht,
  "Operation Eisenfaust" and every other episode, level or character name. The mode's own name
  (`MODE_TITLE`) must not follow that pattern either: no "OPERATION <German word>", no
  "CASTLE …". (The plain words "guard", "officer", "SS", "dog" are the genre's; for the boss name
  see the user-decided exception above.)
- **Maps:** no floor plan, room shape or secret placement from any of its levels (the first
  floors are the best known: no prison-cell start, no copied cell block or first corridors).
  Our start is a wrecked freight car; the floors are an industrial supply depot and a hangar.
- **Art:** no sprite, texture, HUD layout (no status bar with the face portrait in the middle and
  its order of boxes), face portrait, weapon view, item or treasure design (no cross, chalice,
  chest or crown treasure; no dog-food bowl, turkey dinner, white first-aid kit with a red cross
  or blue extra-life sphere; never the protected Red Cross emblem at all), no
  recreated stone-and-eagle walls, cell walls or portrait walls. Its mech boss's look is not ours.
- **Sound and music:** no track, melody or sound effect, not even "in the style of" a named track.
  Prompts never name the game, its composer or its tracks.
- **Voice lines:** none of its shouts or death lines, e.g. "Achtung!", "Halt!", "Schutzstaffel!",
  "Spion!", "Alarm!", "Mein Leben!", "Mutti!", "Mein Gott!", "Guten Tag!", "Scheiße!",
  "Die, Allied Schweinehund!", "Eva, auf Wiedersehen!" — and no close variant of them. Our German
  lines are in spec §7.4 and §9.3.
- **Text:** no menu or screen text, e.g. "Get Psyched!", "Let's see that again!", the difficulty
  names ("Can I play, Daddy?", "Don't hurt me.", "Bring 'em on!", "I am Death incarnate!"), its
  floor-complete wording (our tally heading is `<FLOOR NAME> CLEARED`) or its end texts. Our
  cards, tally lines and difficulty labels stay the game's own (RECRUIT / VETERAN / COMMANDO).
