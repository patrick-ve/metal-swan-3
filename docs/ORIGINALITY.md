# Originality rules — Metal Swan 3

**Metal Swan 3** is an *original* game. It borrows only the **genre mechanics** of 1990s arcade
run-and-gun shooters (which are not protected), never the protected **expression** of any
existing game. Every agent must follow these rules for names, art, text, sound and level design.

## Allowed (genre mechanics and conventions)

- Side-scrolling run-and-gun play, one-hit deaths, lives and continues, score.
- Weapon crates with letter icons and limited ammo; grenades; automatic melee at close range.
- Tied-up captives that the player frees and who hand over items.
- Vehicles the player can enter, with their own weapons and armour; ejecting.
- Branching routes, checkpoints, timers, camera locks for arena fights, multi-part bosses.
- 2-player co-op, comedic tone, cartoon (bloodless) deaths, a military setting.
- Generic weapon types and names: pistol, machine gun, rocket launcher, shotgun, flamethrower,
  laser, homing missiles, grenades, knife.
- Generic phrases such as "MISSION 1", "START", "MISSION COMPLETE", "GAME OVER", "CONTINUE?".

## Not allowed (protected expression)

Do not use, imitate or closely paraphrase any of the following from the *Metal Slug* series
(SNK / Nazca) or from any other franchise:

- **Names:** Metal Slug, SNK, Nazca, Marco, Tarma, Eri, Fio, Peregrine Falcons, Sparrows,
  Morden, Rebel Army, Regular Army, Ptolemaic Army, Mars People, Rootmars, Allen O'Neil,
  Hyakutaro, Rumi Aikawa, Huge Hermit, Sol Dae Rokker, Jupiter King, Monoeye, Hi-Do, Big Shiee,
  Tani Oh, Iron Nokana, Aeshi Nero, Girida-O, Di-Cokka, Bradley, Sarubia, SV-001, Slug Flyer,
  Slug Mariner, Elephant Slug, Camel Slug, Ostrich Slug, Donkey Slug, Drill Slug, Slug Copter,
  Astro Slug, LV Armor, or any "<animal> Slug" vehicle naming.
- **Character designs:** no look-alikes of the series' heroes, villains or enemy soldiers (for
  example: no bearded captives in underwear, no green-fatigue soldier with the series' insignia,
  no tentacled alien with a ray gun, no round tank with a top vulcan and the series' silhouette).
  Use the project's own character, faction and vehicle designs from `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`.
- **Signature gags and transformations:** no zombie / mummy / fat transformations and no
  copies of specific set pieces, boss designs or level layouts.
- **Text and voice:** no copied announcer lines, captions, story text or dialogue. Write new lines.
- **Music and sound:** compose new melodies, harmonies and rhythms. Never transcribe or
  "arrange" an existing tune from any game, film or song. No sampled or recorded audio.
- **Art:** never trace, copy, or reproduce sprites, logos, fonts or tiles from any game. All art
  is generated by this project's own code from its own palette.

## Self-check before you finish

1. Would a fan of the original series recognise a *specific* character, vehicle, boss, level,
   tune or line? If yes, change it until they would not.
2. Are all proper names invented for this project? (Search your files for the banned names.)
3. Is every melody something you composed for this task?

The project's own identity: the player squad, the enemy faction, vehicles, bosses, missions and
story are defined in `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md`. The title is **METAL SWAN 3**.
