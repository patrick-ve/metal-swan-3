Amsterdam in Depth

Today every plane of ams2 has the same contrast and the same colour strength. A house across the canal looks as near as a bench on the quay. This proposal adds atmospheric perspective: the farther a plane is, the more it fades into the air of that hour. Drag across a screen to compare. Left is the level now. Right is the proposal.

Darks lift firstFar shadows take the cool tone of the air. The outline becomes a coloured line.
Lights turn warmFar highlights lean to the low warm tone of the sun. Contrast gets small.
Colour fadesEach plane loses colour strength in step with its distance.
Detail dropsThe two farthest planes use flat tones only. A mist band hides their feet.
Near stays strongThe walk plane does not change. The nearest strip gets deeper darks.

How it goes into the game

  • The haze is a colour table per plane. The art generator applies it when it paints a layer. There is no alpha, no shader and no cost per frame.
  • New layers per section: one far skyline, two cloud sheets and one or two mist bands. Each is a tileable generator layer, as the others are.
  • The sky becomes one ramp of 23 tones with an ordered dither at the band borders. The clouds use 5 tones and a lit rim.
  • Ground planes fade with distance. The canal, the river and the lawn go from the haze tone at the far edge to full colour at the near edge.

What does not change

  • Figures, enemies, props, hitboxes, terrain and scripts.
  • The painted floor in three bands, and the walk band.
  • The post rule: Amsterdammertjes stand on kerbstones only.
  • The hero method for the middle and near planes: outline, selout, hard clusters.

Decisions for you

  • Dither. The style contract says no dither and no gradient. The sky, the mist and the ground fade break that rule on purpose. Is that correct?
  • Smaller far banks. Stage a is 15 % lower and stage c is 20 % smaller, so the sky and the skyline show. The collision does not change.
  • Haze strength. The numbers in the tables are a first setting. One factor per stage can raise or lower them.

These frames come from the Python art spike of ams2 at the 4× tier, not from the game. The speed in the game is not measured yet.
