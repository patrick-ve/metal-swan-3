# RATTENJONG MET DE RATTENITEMS — level card (brainstorm, 2026-11-11; approved by the user 2026-11-11)

Pitch:        The squad clears De Wallen by night of shady drug dealers and pimps, then goes down into the
              Amsterdam sewers, where gigantic rats attack; at the end they defeat the rat boss, the Rattenjong,
              who throws items from the sewer at the players.
The picture:  The Rattenjong on his trash throne: a huge sewer hall, a mountain of trash (bikes, fatbike wheels,
              shopping carts) that he throws at the squad; green water glows under the grates.
Place in the campaign: level 2 (MISSION 2), after `ams`, before `rally`; replaces `oat`, which is deleted.
Tone:         sleazy neon above (loud pimps, whispering dealers), gross-out cartoon horror below (slime, trash,
              smell lines, huge rats). Bubbles and voices in plain Dutch (generated voices); cards and HUD English.
Never:        sex workers shown, targeted or joked about (windows: closed curtains, red tubes, ground floor only);
              can collectors (homeless people) in the level; drug use shown; real names or brands.
              Bloodless: hits on rats splash green slime.
The new thing: flashlights in the dark sewer: each hero has a light cone; rats are red eyes until the light hits
              them — stays: the five heroes and their weapons, the euro budget; delivery riders and fatbike youths
              return. No Metal Swan, no handhavers.
Journey:      a DE WALLEN, the quay, 23:00 → b alleys and the church square, 01:00 (the rats come up) → c the drop
              down the burst drain shaft into a brick tunnel, 01:30 (flashlights on) → d the sewer tunnels, 02:30
              (the fatberg) → e the trash hall under a canal, 05:00 (the Rattenjong) → the squad climbs out of a
              manhole far from the centre at dawn (07:15), covered in slime; a taxi waits; cut to the A10 race.
              No split; about 5 minutes.
Scenes:
  a  De Wallen quay, 23:00, autumn drizzle (late October). Key: red and pink neon on wet blue-black; based on the
     approved De Wallen night scene of the canal study. Set piece: GOUDEN GERRIT's white limousine across a bridge
     (mini-boss, camera lock). Ground: wet cobbled quay with kerb and bike rack, the canal beside it, mirroring the
     neon. Far side: the opposite quay with gabled fronts; the old church tower floodlit above the roofs (backdrop
     only). Local things: green curl urinals by the canal; bikes chained to the bridge rails; closed windows with
     red tubes on the ground floor only; puddles that mirror the neon; a moored boat under a tarp; passers-by with
     umbrellas. Moves: rain, rings on the canal, neon flicker, the reflections.
  b  Narrow alleys, then the square by the old church, 01:00, rain. Key: the same neon, colder and darker in the
     alleys; one warm wall lamp per alley. Set piece: the rat eruption — every drain grate on the square bursts, a
     flood of rats pours out, dealers and pimps run (camera lock, the first rat fight). Ground: alley cobbles with
     a central gutter; the paved square. Far side: brick walls close behind in the alleys; the church wall with
     buttresses (backdrop only, never marked by shots). Local things: drain grates that rattle before they burst;
     roller shutters; bins with cans; steam from a vent; a krul at the alley mouth. Moves: rain, steam, the grates.
  c  The burst drain shaft and the first brick tunnel, 01:30. A short in-engine fall down the shaft (rats on the
     walls), then a horizontal tunnel where the flashlights switch on and the player learns them. Key: dark
     teal-green; pink and red light shafts from the grates above; warm white flashlight cones. Ground: the narrow
     walkway beside the channel of dark water. Local things: iron rungs, pipes, rain falling in through the
     grate, drips, moss, red eyes in side pipes. Moves: the falling water, drips, blinking eyes.
  d  The sewer tunnels, 02:30. Old brick vaults: round arches, the channel in the middle, walkways on both sides,
     iron ladders, street grates above. Key: as c, with the faint green glow of the water. Set piece: a fatberg as
     big as the tunnel, full of wet wipes and rats; shoot it apart (fire burns it faster) while rats burst out
     (camera lock). Far side: side arches and side tunnels in perspective, with red eyes. Local things: fatbergs
     (some hide rats or pickups); lost things in the water (phones, keys, dentures, a selfie stick, a bike); red
     eyes in side pipes; outfall pipes pouring; rusty ladders. Moves: the water flow, floating lost things, light
     shafts with rain, the eyes.
  e  The trash hall under a canal, 05:00. A wide brick hall; its vault cracks and leaks; bikes, scooters and
     shopping carts from the canal bottom sink through and pile up into the Rattenjong's throne. Key: green water
     glow below, cold grey light and dripping water from the cracks above. Local things: the trash mountain (bikes,
     fatbike wheels, carts, scooters); GOUDEN GERRIT's gold chain on the throne (gag); water jets from the cracks;
     rats on the pile; lost things. Moves: falling trash, leaking water, the pile shifting.
  end  A manhole far from the centre at dawn, 07:15: cold pale pink and blue sky after the rain; the squad climbs
     out covered in slime; a taxi waits.
Cast:         returning: delivery riders, fatbike youths (street) · new: dealers (new design), pimps (loud suit, gold
              chains, a cane; mixed look pool), GOUDEN GERRIT's bodyguards, runner rat, leaper rat, spitter rat,
              big brute rat · hostages: tied-up tourists (a stag-party guy in a costume) in the street, a sewer
              worker in orange overalls in the sewer (gives a weapon or a fresh flashlight battery) · mini-boss:
              GOUDEN GERRIT at his white limousine on the bridge (sunroof, gold shotgun, bodyguards; flees when the
              limo is wrecked) · boss: the Rattenjong, a giant rat king on a trash throne: 1 throws bikes, carts and
              scooters; 2 calls rat waves, the throne shrinks; 3 jumps down and charges; a hit cracks the canal
              roof and the flood washes him away down the sewer (bloodless).
Sound:        four songs: street sleazy synth-funk, sewer drones and drips with a low pulse, the GOUDEN GERRIT loop,
              the heavy grimy boss track · Dutch voice lines (generated) · the sound of the place: rain on cobbles
              above, drips and rat squeaks below · ElevenLabs for songs, sfx and voices, cap 35,000 credits,
              estimate first.
Scope:        5 sections; new figures: dealer, pimp, bodyguard, 4 rats, GOUDEN GERRIT, the Rattenjong, the sewer
              worker; new mechanic: flashlights (a light mask); set pieces: the limo, the rat eruption, the drop, the
              fatberg, the flood · the guide's six stops · looks on private pages, play in the running game ·
              helpers as the guide says (count told before each wave) · `oat` deleted in Phase 4 · no deadline named.
