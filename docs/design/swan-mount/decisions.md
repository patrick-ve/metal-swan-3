# SWAN-3 "Metal Swan" as a ridden mount — decisions

> **Date:** 2026-10-09. **Owner:** the GDD integration step. **Inputs:** `feel.md`, `look.md` and
> `levels.md` in this folder (read in full) and the user's request: a ridden "Metal Swan" in the
> role of the reference game's ostrich mount (fast, very high jumps, an aimable gun).
> **Applied to:** `docs/GAME_DESIGN.md` §1, §2, §3.6, §4, §6.3, §8, §9.4, §9.5, §10.1, §10.3,
> §11, §12.1, §13, §14, §15 and §16.1.
> **Rule used:** feel owns logic numbers (speeds, boxes, timings); look owns art (look, sizes,
> frame points, strip names, rider frames); levels owns layouts. Where two inputs disagree, the
> owner wins unless a line below says why not. Units: px, ticks (60 per second), px/tick.

One line per decision: what was decided, then the reason.

1. **Role:** the Swan is the fastest ride and the best jumper, ridden on her back; this reverses the GDD canopy rule. Reason: the user's request overrides the originality critic's canopy rule; riding a creature-shaped robot is a genre mechanic (GDD §16.1).
2. **Run speed 2.6 px/tick** (trot 1.5 after 6 ticks, full speed after 25 ticks and 45 px), not the levels target of 2.4. Reason: feel owns it; 2.6 beats the van (2.4) so she is clearly the fastest ride, stays under the camera's 3 px/tick limit, and lies inside the levels' ±15 % band (2.04–2.76).
3. **Jump vy −5.9 with her own gravity 0.18: apex 5.9² / 0.36 = 96.7 px [93.8], fixed height, no cap.** Reason: feel owns it ("jumps very high" = 2.05 × on foot); look's earlier "cap 85–88 px" is gone from the current look.md, which shows the rider's head in view at the apex from y 176 (y 10–14 [engine]).
4. **Air speed cap = clamp(take-off speed, 1.5, 2.6).** Reason: hopping must never beat running or swimming (feel §4.3).
5. **GLIDE adopted** (hold jump while falling; one per airtime; 60 ticks; fall ≤ 1.0; speed ≤ 2.2; never gains height). Reason: a swan-only verb that is not the reference mount's; it keeps the tap-jump tempo and averages 2.3 px/tick, slower than running (feel §5). *Review: the opening flap lifts her at most 1.3 px [engine 1.1] above the apex; a sprint jump + full glide is 268 px in 112 ticks with forward held (Review fixes, F11-jump-glide-text).*
6. **Float line = surface + 16 px.** Reason: feel and the current look.md §13.2 agree; levels' second value (30 px) came from an older look draft, and every levels reach check passes with both.
7. **Swim 2.0 px/tick, pivot turn, 8-tick paddle-up, duck in water.** Reason: feel owns it; jump-hopping from water then averages exactly the swim speed ((130 + 16) / 73 = 2.0).
8. **Shallow water: she wades at full speed** where the ground is less than 16 px under the surface. Reason: feel §3.8; long legs; it also makes the m1-c slipway work to x 880.
9. **"The pilot cannot be hit" (GDD 8.1) is kept;** the rider's box is part of her hurtbox. Reason: feel argues for it (one rule for every vehicle; a fragile mount would betray the fantasy; the base class already protects the driver), and the task said to keep it unless feel argued otherwise.
10. **The 8.1 "head and shoulders in the cockpit" line is now vehicle-specific:** `mount` pose for the van and the Bandwagon, astride and visible from the hips up for the Swan (and the Trumpeter). Reason: task instruction; look §6.
11. **Rider art: look's 9 `ride-*` strips (14 frames, hip anchor) plus `Player.drawRide`,** instead of feel's single `mount-duck` frame and the 8-strip list in levels D7. Reason: look owns rider art; its `ride-duck` covers the flat crouch, and levels' list came from an older look draft (it named `ride-skid` and lacked `ride-hunch` / `ride-duck`).
12. **Crouched seat point (rider hip) at (−4, −16), deck + 4 px,** not feel's (−4, −20); the logic crouch box stays (−22, −20, 40, 20). Reason: the flat rider must sink to show the crouch; the logic box decides hits; deck + 6 px would need new arm targets (look §6.4, §17 open point).
13. **Water-duck rider box (−10, −37, 12, 15) added** next to feel's ducked hull box (−22, −24, 40, 12). Reason: the hunched rider is visible there, so honest hits need a box; chest-high shots (22.5–25.5 px above the water) still pass 1.5 px over its 21 px top (look §13.6).
14. **Muzzle points follow the art:** 0° (40, −24), −45° (35, −58), −90° (14, −76), −135° (−12, −78), +45° (35, −15), crouched (38, −14), swimming (42, −23), ducked (40, −18). Reason: feel §7.3 itself says art frame points win; the −135° head must pass 4 px over the hunched rider's hat.
15. **The −112.5° sweep frame (beak tip 81 px up) is accepted.** Reason: the beak is never a hurtbox and the frame shows only 2 ticks per sweep (look §17 open point).
16. **Step cadence: one run frame per 6 px, a foot contact every 24 px** (16 ticks at 1.5, 9.2 at 2.6), not feel's 12 / 8 ticks. Reason: sounds and dust come from contact frames, and an odometer keeps the planted foot from skating (look §13.4).
17. **Size: 77 px long, 68–72 px tall with the rider,** not levels' "about 80×64". Reason: look measured it on the rig.
18. **Look details from look.md** (bow number `3` on the breast, far wing arched behind the rider, near wing flush, lens eye with a shutter lid), not levels' draft Look row (a `3` on the rump, wings standing beside the seat). Reason: look owns the look.
19. **Sprite strip names from look §9** (`waddle`, `paddle-up`, `settle`, `<anim>-front`, neck strips), not levels D41 (`crouch-walk`, `water-takeoff`). Reason: look owns the sprite contract.
20. **TRUMPETER: rider astride, about 76×64 with the rider, hurtbox still the hull 28×14,** not levels' 72×58. Reason: look §14 measured it; shoot-em-up fairness keeps the small hurtbox.
21. **The Golden Goose (Mk2) keeps its enclosed canopy;** feel's option "its pilot rides on its back" is not taken. Reason: look §16.9; the canopy becomes the enemy's own trait, which separates hero and enemy machines; Gramps' new line `MK2 HAD A CANOPY. IT FOGGED UP.` explains the open seat.
22. **SWAN DIVE costs 1 cell; dazed 180 ticks at ≥ 2 cells; migrates (lost) at 1 cell; refused in or over water.** Reason: feel §9.2; her own exit instead of the reference mount running off; a popped-up pilot over water would drown. *Review: "in or over water" is now defined: the dive starts only on ground or a platform and is refused while she swims or is in the air (Review fixes, F5-dive-where).*
23. **Ram buttons stay fire + jump (GDD 4.1).** The code's `TUNING.vehicle.ramButtons = ['jump', 'bomb']` is listed in GDD 15.9 for the build team. Reason: feel follows the GDD; this step may not edit `src/`. *Review: superseded. The code already has `['fire', 'jump']`; the 15.9 note is gone, and the combo window is now the same tick or 1 tick apart (Review fixes, F8 / F5 / F6-stale-ram-note, F1-combo-window).*
24. **Eject only on the ground or in water; in the air a jump press is the glide.** Reason: feel §9.1; the two inputs would clash. *Review: the Swan eject now also needs jump within 4 ticks after down; a jump from a held crouch or duck is a jump (Review fixes, F3-crouch-eject). In water she catches her pilot (F1).*
25. **0 cells: the shared 8.1 rule, plus a cosmetic sooty migration (`migrate-soot`, priority 2).** Reason: feel §9.3 and look §9; she never dies on screen (tone, GDD 1.2), and gameplay does not change.
26. **Contact: stomp only; no trample while ridden.** Reason: feel §8; the van and the Bandwagon own the squash, and the jump is her verb.
27. **Co-op: the PERCH (14×4 one-way tail platform, off from the squat to the landing); no pillion seat.** Reason: feel §10; it reuses dynamic solids and needs no new seated aiming art. *Review: over water it stays on through the jump and carries the partner (Review fixes, F6 / F3 / F2-perch-water).*
28. **Camera: request lead 64 and max speed 4 while a Swan is ridden, and view clamping for every driven ground vehicle.** Reason: feel §11 (180 → 206 px of space ahead; catch-up 323 → 93 ticks; a driven vehicle could leave a locked arena); levels work with either lead. The Trumpeter keeps its own 4 px rule.
29. **BEAK SHELL adds her vx (×1).** Reason: feel §7.5; without it a sprinting Swan runs into her own shell.
30. **HONK numbers unchanged; in the air she hangs (vy 0) for the 10-tick wind-up.** Reason: feel §7.6; the wind-up matches look's `honk` strip (4 + 3 + 3 ticks).
31. **Sound ids: feel's 12 new `swan/*` ids; `swan/step` gets a new recipe (a quick paddle slap, not a heavy stomp); `swan/flap` has its own id but may share the `trumpeter/flap` recipe.** Reason: she is now a fast runner; one id per event keeps the sfx index honest.
32. **`m1-c` re-laid as levels §3.2** (DRUM LINE, slipway, judges' box, BOAT HOP, TIMPANI MINEFIELD, Mk1 stomp gag, ENCORE wall). Reason: the old river was built for a 1.0 px/tick swimmer; the on-foot path is unchanged.
33. **The `m1-c` @1900 rowboat keeps the old wording "from `R+48`", without levels' "to x 2072".** Reason: x 2072 lies under the willow bridge (1950–2110) and overlaps the willow boat at x 2060; see the open questions. *Review: superseded. The rowboat is placed at x 2350, right of island 2 (Review fixes, F4 / F16-rowboat-1900).*
34. **`drum-mine` hurtbox 18×12 at y 171–183, fixed (the bob is cosmetic),** not levels' 18×10 centred on y 178. Reason: the Swan's swimming beak fires at y 173 (art muzzle); a 10 px box from y 173 that bobs ±2 px would be missed at the low point of the bob. On-foot margins stay the same (the duck's top 165 is 6 px above 171).
35. **The ENCORE arena wall at x 2844 is a body wall** (a clamp like the view edges), not a `SolidBox`. Reason: projectiles sweep against dynamic solids, so a solid wall would stop every shot at the bow (x 2848) and make the horn unhittable.
36. **The willow crushes only enemies; players and Swans under it are pushed out unhurt.** Reason: levels D25; a swimming Swan's 48 px body reaches y 148, so the bridge at y 170 would destroy a vehicle for a set piece the player triggered.
37. **`m3-a` re-laid as levels §4.2** (pond 1340–1700, piste with moguls and boulders, low kicker lip at y 190, landing valley at y 204, K-lines, checkpoints 1300 and 2300). Reason: from the old ramp top (y 120) her top half left the view for about 40 ticks at the climax. *Review: one boulder only; goats 45 ticks apart; a baton-copter at @1700; the cliff wall after the UNLOCK (Review fixes, F2, F3, F12, F8-cliff-eject).*
38. **Avalanche pursuit rule adopted:** front += clamp(min(view left + 40, rearmost player x − 104) − front, 1.0, 3.0). Reason: a fixed 1.0 px/tick wall cannot threaten a 2.6 px/tick Swan, and a faster fixed wall would kill players on foot; the rule holds view x 32–40 and gains only at 1.0 px/tick.
39. **Ski jump scored by K-lines** (on foot past blue x 2420; riding, wings opened, past red x 2480; no hit in the air). Reason: a distance alone cannot separate "no glide at the high corner" (2545) from "weakest glide at the low corner" (2499); the wings rule works across the whole band. *Review: superseded. The red K-line is at x 2540, with the tuning floor (Review fixes, F9, F10-tuning-floor).*
40. **SHE TOOK THE LIFT (`m3-c`) adopted.** Reason: a cheap payoff (one decor, one runtime record, one start row, no new swan art or sound); m3 then follows the vehicle → boss rule; VEHICLE AT THE FINISH becomes possible in m3; players without a swan fight on foot as before.
41. **No other new swan appearances** (no route branch, no vertical section, no attract demo). Reason: levels §5.1; a 97 px jump flattens on-foot platforming built on 36–48 px steps, and the van and the Bandwagon own m2 and m4. *Review: an attract demo that rides is now a later request in GDD 15.9 item 5 (Review fixes, F15-attract-demo); one baton-copter joins the m3-a avalanche run (F12).*
42. **Levels' open conflicts (§7.2) are closed by the current look.md:** float line 16 px in both, apex 93.8 with no cap, the flat `ride-duck` crouch (head top 23–25 px). Reason: look.md was revised after levels.md was written.
43. **Seat (mount) zone: feel's rects** (parked (−10, −32, 20, 16), in water (−10, −44, 20, 16)). Reason: feel owns logic boxes; look's art-derived rect differs by 4 px and look accepts either (§13.8).
44. **The egg gag becomes a riveted ivory capsule that pops open into `pickup-bombs`.** Reason: look §10.10; it keeps the GDD gag and passed look's check against famous egg-laying mounts (she never throws eggs or eats enemies).
45. **P2's swan is "the Mute" on the ground too** (`params.variant: 'mute'`). Reason: look §12.1; it matches the Mute TRUMPETER of m5.
46. **Title scene shows Grace still on the swimming Swan (`ride-sit`, relaxed S-neck);** HOW TO PLAY adds `HOLD JUMP   GLIDE (SWAN)` (9 lines, the last at y 136). Reason: look §16.7 and levels D35–D36; the ridden swan must appear in the game's key image and the glide needs one line of instruction.
47. **GDD 1.5 rule 5: m2 adds "a heavy vehicle" instead of "a fast vehicle".** Reason: the Swan (2.6) is now faster than the van (2.4).
48. **The ending's Splashdown shows the Cygnets on the Trumpeter (one on the seat, three hanging on).** Reason: with an open seat there is no cabin to sit "in"; it keeps the scene and adds a gag.
49. **New GDD text calls the Swan "she".** Reason: the GDD's own lines already do (`BE GENTLE WITH HER!`, `SHE DOESN'T DO CLIFFS.`), and all three specs do.
