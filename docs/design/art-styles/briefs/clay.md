- Proportions: a bit stubby and rounded; the head slightly big, the hands mitten-like, the
  boots round; every part a soft, rounded volume (no sharp corners in the silhouette).
- Soft, smooth shading with a broad glossy highlight on each volume (vinyl) or a matte sheen
  with faint fingerprint / tool marks (clay) — pick one and commit.
- Soft ambient occlusion where parts meet; a soft contact shade between the limbs.
- Saturated toy colours (the tweed becomes a warm brown "fabric" sculpted with a few grooves,
  the tie a glossy red ribbon); the face sculpted: a raised brow ridge, a smirk carved in.
- A thin dark outline or none — what reads best on the street.
