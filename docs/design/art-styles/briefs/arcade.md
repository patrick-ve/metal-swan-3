# Your style: `arcade` — ARCADE 90S

The confident hand-pixeled look of a 90s arcade cabinet sprite, rebuilt for 4K.
- Proportions: a little chunky and heroic. The head about 15 % bigger, big hands and boots,
  a sturdy stance; the gun reads big and heavy.
- A crisp, thick dark outline (about 1 logical px, so 4 px at 4×), coloured by the part
  (dark brown around the tweed, deep olive around the trousers), black only where needed.
- Cel shading in 3–4 hard bands per material with HUE SHIFT: shadows go cooler and toward
  purple / blue, highlights go warmer and toward yellow. Saturated, punchy colours.
- Crisp 1-px specular glints on the boots, the gun, the buckle and the hair; no soft gradients.
- Faces read at a glance: a strong brow, a bold smirk, the stubble as a clean dark band.
