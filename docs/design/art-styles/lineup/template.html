Vincent in Six Styles

One player, six art directions, each drawn by its own renderer over the same rig. Every clip is the same play in the test arena at the 4K art: stand, run, jump and flip, shoot, aim up, crouch and fire. Only the drawing changes.

The close-up follows Vincent. Use Enlarge to see one style big, or slow the clock down to read single frames.
