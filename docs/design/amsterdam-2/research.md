## 1. The havermelkelite: who they are, status symbols, rituals

- The term "havermelkelite" (oat-milk elite) comes from journalist Jonas Kooyman: a book *De havermelkelite: hoe de nieuwe yup de stad onherkenbaar verandert*, an Instagram account and a newsletter. Profile: 20s–30s, work at start-ups, in the creative sector or at the Zuidas; €9 sourdough, orange wine, €3,000 e-bikes, coffee out every day with oat milk, pricey gyms, weekend restaurants and clubs. [E] [R: the author is a real person; use the word, never him] https://www.deslegte.com/de-havermelkelite-3590075/
- His checklist (RTL Nieuws via DutchReview, 2024-07-30): academic background, a formerly working-class neighbourhood in the Randstad, votes progressive-left, **Adidas Sambas or Birkenstock Bostons**, oat-milk cappuccinos, e-bike, **identical stainless-steel tables**, **the same Spotify playlist in every coffee bar**, restaurants found **via TikTok/Instagram**, yoga / pilates / a class app, open relationship or a dating app. Quote: "Everything and everyone is starting to look the same"; advice: "dare to admit that you also find all the things you buy ridiculously expensive". [E] https://dutchreview.com/?p=213304
- ELLE (2023-10-29): Salomon sneakers, Aesop soap in the bathroom, hot yoga or spinning at the weekend, creative / marketing / PR / Zuidas jobs, a daily oat-milk coffee, **queueing for bakeries at the weekend**, sourdough (for "gut health"), natural and orange wine, cruffins; lives mainly in **Oud-West and Noord**; swims at the Marineterrein; Fort Negen is "a well-known bakery with long queues". [E] https://www.elle.be/nl/?p=363928
- Hart van Nederland (2024-10-26, Amsterdam 750): bakfietsen, oat-milk cappuccinos, natural wine, €7/h parking; a sociologist: coffee used to be simple milk coffee in a brown café, now "all kinds of choices". The group is seen as a threat to the "authentic" Amsterdam feeling. [E] https://www.hartvannederland.nl/milieu-gezondheid/ontspanning/artikelen/van-koffie-halen-in-kroeg-naar-havermelkcappu-yuppenstad-amsterdam-750-jaar

## 2. Queues and hotspots

- TikTok queues (2023): Fabel Friet's line can cross the Keizersgracht; the owner employs **two permanent security guards** for the line; customers order by QR code while they wait; Van Stapele cookies (~100 people, waiting before opening); Chun Café has a crowd manager. [N 2023, E] https://dutchreview.com/featured/tiktok-tourists-take-over-amsterdam/ · https://www.welingelichtekringen.nl/samenleving/de-tiktok-rij-sneue-toeristen-staan-in-amsterdam-uren-te-wachten-op-friet-of-een-stroopwafel
- Street-sign protest (2023-05-18): pranksters renamed **Berenstraat → Schapenstraat (Sheep Street)** and **Runstraat → Rijstraat (Queue Street)** with stickers; "tourists behave like sheep", aimed at the behaviour, not the shops. [N 2023, gag is E] https://nltimes.nl/node/66232
- 2026: University of Amsterdam research: TikTok-driven visitors to the Negen Straatjes now come **year-round**, not in seasonal peaks; "What you see in the city in terms of lines is the outcome of an algorithm." [N 2026-04-07] https://nltimes.nl/node/87889 · (a June 2026 Tilburg paper on the same theme: https://research.tilburguniversity.edu/en/publications/from-tiktok-feeds-to-food-queues-social-media-induced-food-touris/)
- **Fort Negen** (Jan Evertsenstraat 31, Amsterdam-West): sandwich bar + bakery, "a queue almost always forms outside the door"; a viral **cheese-soufflé brioche** on TikTok/Instagram; almond croissants and cruffins; deliveries via an online supermarket deal. [E] [R: a real business] https://www.linda.nl/eten-recept/wat-goeeeed/fort-negen-crisp/ · https://www.yourlittleblackbook.me/de/hotspots-jan-evertsenstraat-amsterdam/ · https://wanderlog.com/place/details/2349528

## 4. Traffic, vehicles and fines

- **Brommobielen (the 45 km/h micro-cars, e.g. the Biro):** from age 16 with a moped licence; Amsterdam went from ~1,200 (2020) to **4,700+ (end 2025)** thanks to a city-wide parking-permit trial; a new parking policy at the end of 2025 made sales **collapse** in Amsterdam (they now pay to park outside their own district). [N 2026-03] https://www.accountant.nl/nieuws/2026/3/verkoop-brommobielen-groeit-sneller-dan-ooit-maar-stort-in-amsterdam-in/
- Driver image: "influencers with a rain allergy, or people who don't want to park their Panamera in the city"; complaints about parking on the pavement. [N 2019, image is E] https://topgear.nl/autonieuws/de-biro-terreur-in-amsterdam-is-binnenkort-afgelopen/

## 5. Tourism and the Wallen

## 8. Sensitivity notes (house rules: cartoon, bloodless, behaviours not groups)

- **Deposit hunters** are often poor or homeless people. Do not make them enemies. Let gulls and rats compete for the cans instead.
- **Street dealers and taxi drivers:** use the normal mixed skin/outfit variant pool (like the youths); the joke is the behaviour (whispering "COKE?", fake pills, refusing short rides, the meter), never an origin.
- **Sex workers** are never targets, never jokes, never shown in windows (closed curtains or neon only). Wallen enemies are dealers, stag parties and pushy crowds.
- **Party tourists:** no national flags or accents as the joke (the stag party already covers this type).
- **Pride / Koningsdag / ADE:** fine as a backdrop or a crowd hazard, never as an enemy group.
- **Real people:** Jonas Kooyman (the term's author) and the YouTuber in the ferry story: never characters. The mayor is only the existing ROBO caricature.
- **Gloat rule:** new enemies may not laugh at, point at or film a player's death (ams-extras §1). Gloat ideas below respect this.

## A. Enemies (15)

1. ★ **BRAMMOBIEL-INFLUENCER** (`brommobiel`) — a 45 km/h cube micro-car with a ring light on the roof and a phone on a gimbal; putters along the bike path, then **parks across the pavement** (a solid block: jump on it or over it); TELL: the ring light charges (24 ticks) → a **flash cone** ahead (a hit; crouch or stay behind the car) + horn — shoot the car (soft shell) and the influencer pops out with a selfie stick and flees — **budget:** the wreck drops `SPONSORDEAL` coins (+€25); a handhaver on screen fines a pavement-parked car €140 and tips you 10 %. Gloat: parks on the spot and scrolls her likes.
2. ★ **TAXI** (`taxi`) — a black electric sedan with a roof sign and a **roof meter that ticks while it is on screen**; swerves from the road onto the bike lane; TELL: hazard lights + horn → either a **door swing** (dooring, knee height: jump) or a ram (jump over) — shoot the meter (a part) to freeze it, or wreck the car — **budget:** if the taxi leaves the screen alive, its meter sum is billed to you (`RIT €38,50`); bubble `TE KORT!` when you stand close (refuses short rides). Gloat: idles at the spot, meter running.
3. ★ **DE FLUISTERAAR** (`straatdealer`, Wallen) — hood up in a dark doorway (mixed variant pool); TELL: the background LCD screen flashes `PAS OP: STRAATDEALERS` and he whispers `COKE? XTC?`; he never shoots: he sidles up and **pickpockets** on contact (no death) — shoot him before he reaches the screen edge to drop the € sack — **budget:** contact = −10 % of the budget; catching him returns it +€5 finder's fee; his pills drop as a fake pickup (`NEP!`, mints).
4. ★ **BAKFIETS-OUDER** (`bakfiets`) — an e-cargo bike pushed like a tank; the box (toddlers under a rain hood) is an **armoured shield** (shots ping; the kids are never hurt); TELL: bell + `AAN DE KANT!` → a charge at 1.6 px/tick (jump the box); the toddlers lob rice cakes (arc) — get behind and shoot the rider; defeat = a flat tyre, the parent pushes it off lecturing `IK STA HIER OOK!` — **budget:** every shot into the box costs €1 `LAKSCHADE`, so flanking pays.

9. **RATTENPLAAG** (`rat`) — at night, rats pour from torn bin bags (Kalverstraat): 5–12 fast one-shot critters that hop over low shots; they **snatch your dropped coins and cans** and run to a drain — shoot fast, or shoot the bag before it tears — **budget:** every coin a rat reaches a drain with is lost. ## B. Mechanics (10 + 1), focus: the BUDGET score

1. ★ **BUDGET (the score)** — the HUD reads `BUDGET € 1.234,56` (Dutch notation); start with `VAKANTIEGELD € 250`; a defeated enemy drops coins worth its old score / 100 (fatbike €3, groom €10); a freed hostage pays a `FOOI`; medals become coins; the end tally is a `JAARREKENING` (lines: statiegeld +, fooien +, boetes −, ritten −, toeristenbelasting −) stamped `OVERSCHOT` or `TEKORT`; high scores = `RIJKSTE TOERISTEN`.
2. ★ **STATIEGELD BINS** — the green city bins (with XXX) burst when shot (2 hits) and spray cans (€0,15) and bottles (€0,15 / €0,25) that bounce and **must be grabbed within ~4 s** before gulls and rats take them; some bins have a **lock** (shoot the lock first); a bag with the sticker `GEEN STATIEGELD` is a jackpot; the yellow `STATIEGELD` bin is a big piñata (€5+).
3. ★ **CONTEXT FINES** — a handhaver's ticket names a real offence and price based on what you do at that moment: on foot on the bike path, a fatbike in the Vondelpark (€115), a brommobiel on the ferry (€140), the flamethrower in the Wallen (`BLOWVERBOD €100`), standing still on a bridge (€190); **a ticket costs euros, not a life**; 3 unpaid tickets → a red `DWANGBEVEL` ticket that does kill; pay them off at a `BOETEBUREAU` kiosk to reset.
4. ★ **TOERISTENBELASTING** (continue cost) — each in-world continue takes 12,5 % of the budget; the rate climbs one step per continue, as in the 2026 coalition plan (16 % → 17 % → … → 20 %); the HUD digit rolls up.
5. ★ **OVERBIEDEN** (the house auction shop) — at a checkpoint a canal house `TE KOOP — VRAAGPRIJS €…`; stand on the doormat to bid; rival bidders in the windows raise the price every second (`+€5.000!`); stay = pay more, leave = `VERKOCHT` slams; win = the door opens on a weapon crate + food (rarely the 1UP); the final price nearly always ends above the sign (86 % in Noord).
6. **INLEVERAUTOMAAT + BLIKKENKANON** — collected cans go into a can counter; cash them at a reverse-vending machine (+bonus per can, the "return bonus" plan) **or** fire them as the special `BLIKKENKANON` (each shot costs €0,15): money or firepower.
7. **PARKEERMETER** — a parked vehicle costs €8,05 per in-game "hour" (e.g. 10 s) while you are off it; a meter post next to it shows the running sum; unpaid at the next scan car = a clamp (reuses `ams-wheel-clamp`).
8. **FIETSDEPOT + MAGNEETVISSEN** — a vehicle left behind is towed (`WEGGESLEEPT`, €25 + €15 to bring it back); or fish it from the canal at a magnet spot: each pull = random loot (bike, shopping cart, scooter, a safe with €€, a rubber duck).
9. **ROOD STAAN** — below €0 the HUD turns red and a **`DEURWAARDER`** (bailiff in grey with a sack) walks in every 30 s; on contact he repossesses your special weapon (back to the pistol); shoot him (HP 30) and bins refill so you can climb out of debt.
10. **HAVERMELK-BAR** (spend shop) — a coffee window sells boosts: flat white €3,95 (+€0,50 oat) = speed and fire rate for 10 s; matcha €6 = slow-motion focus 5 s; a €9 sourdough = full heal; prices rise 10 % per purchase (`INFLATIE`).
