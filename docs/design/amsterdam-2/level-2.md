# Level 2 — OAT OF CONTROL (proposal)

Status: **proposal** (brainstorm with the user, 2026-10-31). Not a spec and not a plan; nothing is
built. The user's choices are marked *(user)*; the rest are lead proposals that fill the gaps and
can change. The idea pool behind it is in `brainstorm.md` (2026-10-29) and `research.md`.

The request: "Based on research done earlier, can you propose a level 2 in Amsterdam? Move the car
race section to a later stage in the game."

## 2. The idea *(user)*

- **Story: een dag uit.** A tourist day in Amsterdam that goes wrong, from morning to night. No
  special goal; the city and its prices are the joke. The squad is still on shore leave after the
  fries of `ams`.
- **Name: OAT OF CONTROL** (a pun on oat milk and the havermelkelite).
- **Route: de binnenstad**, the old centre, day to night, four sections and a boss.
- **Length:** about the same as `ams` (4 sections plus the boss, roughly 12–15 minutes).
- **Opening: een toeristenfolder** *(user, round 7)*: the mission card is a glossy tourist folder
  with the day plan (the 9 Straatjes, the bakery, the canal, the Wallen). Generic city-marketing
  look, never the real city logo or slogan. (Not picked: Gramps's postcard, a parody booking app,
  the normal `ams` card.)

## 3. The sections

| | Section | Time | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| a | **DE 9 STRAATJES** | morning | Boutiques with three items each and a €400 candle. Statiegeld bins, the havermelk-bar shop, the hijsbalk, het draaiorgel. Ends with **De Makelaar** as the mini-boss. |
| b | **FORT NEGEN & ZESTIG** | noon | The parody bakery and its queue: **DE RIJ**, hold your place (camera lock; the queue steps forward every 5 s; line-cutters push you back a place). The blauwe envelop. |
| c | **DE KADEMUUR** | afternoon | **The Swan returns** *(user)*: ride the canal while the old quay wall crumbles behind you (a chase). A closed bridge, pontoon bridges, a sinkhole that swallows a parked scan car. |
| d | **DE WALLEN BY NIGHT** | night | Neon and closed curtains, narrow alleys, dealers, taxis, `BLOWVERBOD` signs, tour groups stopping on bridges. The hijsbalk again. At **03:00 the neon goes out**, the street goes dark, and the boss lights up the house. |

Section details *(user, rounds 6 and 7)*:

- **a — De conceptstore** *(round 7)*: one item on a pedestal, a single sock for €85. Shoot the
  glass case: the sock is a weapon crate in disguise. (Not picked: `U BREEKT, U BETAALT` fragile
  goods, vintage per kilo with hats, the beard barber.)

- **b — U BENT NUMMER 47:** a queue number on screen counts down; each line-cutter the player
  stops moves them up one place. (Not picked: a rain shower with umbrellas as platforms, the
  influencer's photo stop that whites out the screen, the wrong queue for a sneaker drop.)
- **c — an auto-scroll chase:** the screen scrolls forward on the Swan; the quay wall crumbles
  behind the player and bricks splash into the canal; keep up or get hit. (Not picked: two levels
  with attacks from the quay above, the wall falling ahead.)

Lead proposals inside the sections (not decided): the draaiorgel plays once in a, as the first
"free shooting window" lesson; the blauwe envelop floats in at b's queue first and then appears now
and then in later sections; the Swan comes back in c only (d is on foot, the alleys are too narrow).

### Mini-boss: De Makelaar *(user: "Mini-boss in a")*

At the end of DE 9 STRAATJES he sells a canal house. He plants `VERKOCHT` signs as cover, throws
`+10%` bidding paddles and yells `OVERBIEDEN!`. While he lives, 1.5 % of every euro the player picks
up flows to him as `COURTAGE`; his fall returns the pile.

- **De bezichtiging** *(user, round 7)*: he opens a front door and a crowd of about 20 viewers
  rushes out. They are harmless but push the player along the street.
- Not picked: the hammer shock wave, the brochure rain, the asking-price sign that sends him into a
  rage.

### Boss: De Huisjesmelker *(user)*

The slumlord who milks a small house like a cow, at the end of DE WALLEN BY NIGHT.

- **Huurverhoging** *(user's pick of the attacks)*: a rent sign goes up each phase (€1.900,
  €2.400, €3.100). Each rise makes him faster and drains a little budget.
- **De sleutelbos** *(user, round 5)*: he swings a giant ring of keys like a flail, low and then
  high; the player jumps or crouches under it.
- Offered and not picked: the milk bottles, the house on legs, the tenants thrown out of the
  windows (round 3); splitsen (walls that shrink the arena), rolkoffers (rolling suitcases), de
  kelderluiken (whack-a-mole cellar hatches) (round 5). The spec may still need a third move.
- The €1.900 studio gag (picked 2026-10-29) fits the end of the fight: a freed tenant says
  `€1.900 PER MAAND` / `EN GEEN KEUKEN!`, and a microwave dings in the hallway.

### Ending: the taxi ride *(user)*

After the boss, an aggressive taxi picks up the squad. The driver races out of the centre and onto
the A10; the MISSION 3 card shows, and its frame glitches into the 3D race as today
(`rally-glitch.ts`).

- **A playable ride** *(user, round 7)*: a short rail shooter. The players lean out of the taxi's
  windows and shoot the handhavers and brommobielen that chase the taxi to the ring. (Not picked:
  a short scene with a €187,50 fare, no ride.) Lead proposal: the meter still runs, and the fare
  is the last line on the kassabon.

## 5. The budget

- **Game-wide, from `ams` on** *(user)*. In `ams` only the score changes: the HUD shows
  `BUDGET € 1.234,56` and enemies pay euros; the level itself stays as it is.
- **Statiegeld bins** *(user)*: shoot a street bin, it explodes or collapses, cans and bottles fly
  out, bounce, and are picked up for deposit money (can €0.15, small bottle €0.15, big bottle
  €0.25).
- **Havermelk-bar shop** *(user)*: a coffee window sells boosts (flat white = speed and fire rate,
  matcha = slow motion, €9 sourdough = full heal); prices go up 10 % per buy (`INFLATIE`).
- **Blauwe envelop** *(user)*: shoot it open for a refund (+€) or an assessment (−€). The sender is
  generic, never the real agency's logo.
- **Fines** *(user, 2026-10-29)*: the `BOETE` hit still kills, and the death also costs a fine.
- **Toeristenbelasting** *(user, 2026-10-29)*: each continue costs 12.5 % of the budget, rising to
  20 %.
- **Below €0: rood staan + shop closed** *(user)*: the budget may go red and negative; the
  havermelk-bar then refuses the player (`GEWEIGERD`) until the budget is above €0 again. The
  tally stamps `TEKORT` or `OVERSCHOT` (the `JAARREKENING`).
- **HUD: the bank-app look** *(user, round 6)*: a clean `BUDGET € 1.234,56`, green above €0 and
  red below, with small `+€0,15` / `−€95` pop-ups. (Not picked: a receipt strip, arcade digits, a
  parking-meter LCD.)
- **Tally: de kassabon** *(user, round 6)*: a long receipt rolls out with every can, coffee, fine
  and continue, then the total, stamped `OVERSCHOT` or `TEKORT`. (Not picked: a tax-form
  jaarrekening page, a bank statement, an arcade count-up.)
- **Two players: two budgets** *(user, round 5)*. Each player has their own euro number (their
  own fines, pickups and continue costs); the tally compares them. (Not picked: one shared budget,
  shared + a `WIE BETAALT?` bill split.)

## 5a. Music and voices *(user, round 5)*

- **A day-to-night score:** a new song per section — bright for a, tense for the queue in b, a
  chase for c, neon synth for d — plus the boss song. (Not picked: a draaiorgel rave, café jazz
  that hardens through the day, the `ams` sound with new songs.)
- **Everybody speaks:** short Dutch ElevenLabs lines (designed voices, as in `oldenzaal`) for De
  Makelaar, De Huisjesmelker, the taxi driver and the havermelk-bar barista, and also for the
  influencer, the bakfiets-ouder, the dealer and the queue. The speech bubbles stay.

## 6. Rules that carry over

- `brainstorm.md` §3: originality (parody names for brands, real Amsterdam places allowed under the
  `ams` exception), behaviours not groups, sex workers never shown or targeted, deposit hunters
  never enemies, the gloat rule.
- `Fort Negen & Zestig` is a parody name; the bakery staff stay friendly and the joke is the queue.
- Fairness: every new attack has a tell of at least 18 ticks on screen.
