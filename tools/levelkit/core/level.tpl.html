Level 1 Repainted

The six sections of level 1, painted again in the canal look of the four scenes you approved. The layout is the game's own: the same section lengths, the same ground and bridges, the same platforms, the same water, and every decor item at its own x and depth on the street floor. Vincent is unchanged; he walks the game's walk line.

Scale (v2): buildings, trees, lamps and far boats are now drawn at 13 px per metre times the speed of their plane. A storey on the street is about as tall as Vincent; a far-bank storey is 20 px. Posts, bikes, benches and every gameplay piece keep their size.

Street (v3): the street is straight from start to finish. Both kerbs are straight lines, the hero walks on the lane or the cobbles, and lamps, trees and benches stand on the pavements. A lane ends at a straight granite threshold. The side canal of section a has a lift bridge: its deck hangs high between two towers and the hero walks under it. Lamps are 72 px tall again, the height where the game's gulls sit.

Drag the screen or the strip under it to move the camera. Layout draws the game's terrain over the art: yellow ground, cyan platforms, blue water.

Route: a → b → c → (c1 the bridge or c2 the cruise) → d. Each button is as wide as its section is long.

What stays as it was

  • The layout: section widths, ground line, bridge humps, platforms, water and the solid ceiling of the passage come from the game's data.
  • The floor: the game's camera (horizon 60, walk line 176) and its street plan: kerb lines, bike lane, cobbles, bulbs, canals.
  • The decor: every item at its x and depth. Gameplay pieces keep their shape: decks, roofs, awnings, ledges, the passage.
  • The hours: a morning, b noon, c golden afternoon, d sunset.
  • Figures, enemies, pickups and effects are not on this page. Vincent is the game's sprite at its own size.

What is new

  • All scenery is painted again with the kit of the four scenes: the same ramps, key light, air and 5-bit colours.
  • Far planes take the colour of the air. The darkest tones are near.
  • The street floor scrolls row by row, as in the game: far rows slower, near rows faster.
  • Water mirrors the far bank, row by row, in three frames.

Limits

  • This page is a design study. Nothing in the game changed.
  • The bridge decks are drawn in the walk plane. The game draws them in depth on its floor.
  • Boats, carts and stalls stand at their start positions. Nothing moves except clouds and water.
  • At 2× and 4× Vincent's pixels are larger than the pixels of the scenery.
